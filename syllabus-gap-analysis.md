# Biology Syllabus Gap Analysis

**Audit date:** 3 August 2026
**App baseline:** 44 curriculum areas and 438 text lessons in `LessonCatalog.kt`

## Scope and status rules

This is a catalogue-level audit against the current published syllabus structures. It checks whether a required idea has a direct lesson in the app. It does not claim that a matching title alone meets every examination learning outcome.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ Covered | The app has direct lessons for the main required concepts. |
| 🟠 Partial | The app has a related or umbrella lesson, but one or more named mechanisms, examples, calculations, practical skills, or exam outcomes need dedicated content. |
| ❌ Not covered | No direct lesson currently addresses the requirement. |

## Executive summary

| Syllabus | Broad units reviewed | Covered | Partial | Not covered | Main conclusion |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| CBSE Biology XI–XII (2026–27) | 10 | 7 | 3 | 0 | Excellent breadth; several NCERT chapter-specific organisms, mechanisms and practical outcomes need lessons. |
| Andhra Pradesh Intermediate Biology | 10 equivalent NCERT units | 7 | 3 | 0 | AP Intermediate Biology moves to the full NCERT-based syllabus in 2026–27, so the CBSE/NCERT gaps apply. |
| Cambridge IGCSE Biology 0610 (2026–28) | 21 | 14 | 7 | 0 | All broad topics are represented; nutrition, drugs, gas exchange and practical/data outcomes need more explicit coverage. |
| Cambridge AS & A Level Biology 9700 (2025–27) | 19 | 15 | 4 | 0 | Strong conceptual alignment; syllabus-specific physiology, disease examples and quantitative practical work remain partial. |
| IB Diploma Biology, first assessment 2025 | 4 themes | 1 | 3 | 0 | Broad themes are present, but several named SL/HL nodes and the assessed investigation workflow need dedicated lessons. |
| College Board AP Biology (2025–26) | 8 | 8 | 0 | 0 | All eight content units are represented; science-practice and required inquiry-lab depth is a separate implementation gap. |

> Percentages are intentionally omitted. A broad unit can contain many learning outcomes, and counting one matching title as complete would overstate readiness.

## 2. Andhra Pradesh State syllabus

The February 2026 BIEAP notification specifies that Intermediate second-year Botany and Zoology will be taught as **Biology—full NCERT-based syllabus** from academic year 2026–27. First-year science had already moved toward NCERT alignment. Therefore:

| AP State scope | Coverage result |
|---|---|
| Intermediate/BiPC theory | Use the CBSE XI–XII table above; the same seven units are covered and three are partial at broad-unit level. |
| Botany practicals | 🟠 Partial: Microscopy, Histology, Staining, Experimental Design and several plant topics exist, but the board-prescribed experiment list is not represented as a practical sequence. |
| Zoology practicals | 🟠 Partial: Animal diversity, anatomy and histology exist, but specimen/spotter identification, prescribed dissections or virtual equivalents, and record-style practical exercises need mapping. |
| Telugu/English terminology alignment | ❌ Not covered as syllabus localization; current lessons use the English language tag. |
| AP examination blueprints and chapter weightage | ❌ Not covered in the lesson catalogue. This should be a metadata/exam-planner feature rather than new biology theory. |

### AP Biology skills gap

The eight content units are covered, but College Board also requires six science practices and substantial inquiry work. The app has theory for Scientific Method, Biological Data, Statistics Basics and Experimental Design, but it does not yet provide a complete assessed workflow for:

- visual model construction and revision;
- student-generated questions and methods;
- graphing and quantitative data representation;
- statistical tests, especially chi-square;
- evidence-based scientific argumentation;
- a minimum of 25% supervised hands-on inquiry time and at least two labs per big idea.

The supervised laboratory requirement cannot be certified by text lessons alone. The app can support it with lab protocols, simulations, data capture and teacher verification, but the school remains responsible for supervision.
